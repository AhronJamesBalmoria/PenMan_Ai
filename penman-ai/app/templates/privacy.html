Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This notice explains what Penman AI collects and why, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect and why
  • Account data (your email and username) to create and secure your account.
  • Your drafts to save them and to give you feedback and grammar suggestions when you ask.
  • A style profile (tone, register, sentence rhythm and word-choice habits), created automatically when you request feedback so Penman can flag large changes in your writing voice. You can remove it at any time with "Reset baseline".
  • [Basic usage logs] to keep the service secure and fix problems.

We use your data based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Please do not put sensitive information (health, government ID numbers, financial details) in your drafts.

Who receives your text

When you request feedback, your text goes to Groq and OpenRouter (AI feedback) and LanguageTool (grammar checking). Supabase hosts our database. These services may run on servers outside the Philippines. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with your school or instructors.

We do not use your text to train AI models. Each provider has its own rules, so only send text you are comfortable sharing with these services.

How long we keep it

Drafts stay until you delete them. Your style profile stays until you reset it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how it is used, or withdraw your consent. You can delete drafts yourself on the Drafts page and reset your style profile from the feedback panel.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

How we handle your writing

Your drafts are stored in Supabase and are only accessible to your account. When you request feedback, your text is sent to third-party AI providers (Groq, OpenRouter) for analysis, and to LanguageTool for grammar checking. A style profile (tone, register and stylometric patterns such as sentence rhythm and word-choice habits) is also stored so Penman can flag large changes in your writing voice.

What we do not do

We do not use your text to train models. We do not sell your data. Only send text you are comfortable sharing with the services listed above.

Academic integrity

Penman AI is an assistant for grammar and feedback. It does not write essays on your behalf. All submitted work must remain your own.

Your choices

You can delete any draft from your Drafts page. To remove your style profile, use "Reset baseline" in the feedback panel. To ask about your data, contact the app administrator.
